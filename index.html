Research

• I work on planning and control for soft robots combining model-based and learning-based approaches.

• My goal is to leverage the robot's model to generate explosive motions, increase robustness, and improve performance through learning, while ensuring theoretical guarantees.

Modeling Soft Robots & Design

Modeling has no golden rule; control-oriented models are often a good fit.
Multimodal robot design.

Optimal Control

Teaching

I believe that teaching is a vocation, like becoming a priest, and I unfortunately never felt it.
I love working in a team of people, growing, learning, making mistakes, trying, retrying, \(\cdots\) (re)\(^n-\)trying something new. I cannot see myself speaking in front of a class of students and doing that for a living.
